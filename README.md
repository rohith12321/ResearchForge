# ResearchForge

A multi-agent AI research assistant for evidence-grounded scientific literature synthesis.

## Objective

ResearchForge retrieves scientific papers, synthesizes findings, verifies claims against source evidence, identifies disagreements, and proposes potential research gaps.

## Initial research domain

AI/ML research papers.

## Planned workflow

Research Planning → Paper Retrieval → Evidence Extraction → Synthesis → Citation Verification → Critique → Final Report.

## Research objective

Compare a single-pass RAG baseline against a multi-agent workflow, measuring evidence support, citation quality, coverage, latency, and cost.

## Status

Initial development and architecture setup.
